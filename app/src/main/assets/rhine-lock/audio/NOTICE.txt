The 28 rhine_*.wav files in res/raw are byte-for-byte copies of the
corresponding frontend/public/audio/*.wav files. The rhine_ filename prefix
only avoids Android resource name collisions. No re-encoding was performed.

draw.wav, access.wav, assemble.wav, process.wav, warning.wav and reveal.wav
are synthesised by upstream tools/assets/gen_sfx.py, distributed with the
project under GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later. See the accompanying AGPL-3.0.txt.

key1.wav, key2.wav, key3.wav, erase.wav, request.wav, nav.wav, open.wav,
close.wav, choose.wav, switch.wav, toggle.wav, arm.wav, execute.wav,
error.wav, glitch1.wav, glitch2.wav, glitch3.wav, recover.wav, standby.wav,
wake.wav, blip1.wav and blip2.wav originate from Kenney Interface Sounds,
dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

ArchivePreviewAudio.java ports the upstream cue table, random variants,
key/navigation pitch spread and perceptual 42 dB gain curve. The complete
visual demo follows upstream entry, verification, denial, recovery and exit
cues, retimed to the Android visual sequence.

Upstream's optional commercial score bgm-loop.opus is not included.
