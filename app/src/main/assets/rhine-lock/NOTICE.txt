The Android and browser renderers adapt the 400-fin curve, transforms,
assembly, peel, carve, and material from frontend/src/lib/scene/Stage.ts and
shaders.ts. Two complete turns and periodic scan alignment close the tilted
fin frame at the loop boundary. The wallpaper uses a GLES 3 adaptation of the upstream grade
effect for denial, after its initial coherent motion.
The archive uses timing and easing from frontend/src/App.svelte, the card
flight from frontend/src/lib/transition.ts, and visual timing from the
AuthRing.svelte and Warning.svelte components.

The desktop galleries use the upstream card.png, dimensions, corner profile
and orientation basis. Their five-card layout adapts the upstream specimen
stream for portrait and landscape wallpaper, with the upstream flow rate and
endpoint fades. Direct lighting and analytic
softbox reflections use the upstream Stage.ts and tools/assets/gen_studio_hdr.py
as their source.
